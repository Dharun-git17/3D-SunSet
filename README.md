# 🌅 Solstice – 3D Sunset Experience

An immersive, interactive **3D Sunset Website** that brings the beauty of a golden-hour sunset to life through real-time browser rendering.

Explore a stunning 3D ocean environment featuring a glowing sun, animated ocean waves, atmospheric clouds, and beautiful sunset lighting.

## ✨ Features

- 🌞 **Interactive 3D Sun** – Watch the sun move from golden hour to night.
- 🌊 **3D Ocean Environment** – Beautifully rendered ocean waves and reflections.
- ☁️ **Atmospheric Clouds** – Stylized 3D clouds across the sunset sky.
- 🎨 **Dynamic Lighting** – Warm sunset colors and realistic lighting effects.
- 🖱️ **Interactive Experience** – Scroll to control the sun's position.
- ▶️ **Auto Play Mode** – Experience the sunset automatically.
- 💻 **Browser-Based Rendering** – No pre-recorded video; the environment is rendered live.
